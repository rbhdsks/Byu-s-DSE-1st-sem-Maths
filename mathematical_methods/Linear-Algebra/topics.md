# Topics: Mathematical Methods / Linear Algebra

[Roadmap](README.md) · [Difficulty and priority](priority.md)

This is the complete study-topic inventory extracted from the uploaded L1, L2, L3, L4, L5 and L6 slides. Related names are grouped into a study unit; every unit has a source range. IDs are reused in `priority.md`. **Orientation** is background mentioned in the slides, **Practice** is an exercise/example block, and **Alternate proof** is an extra route shown in the slides. None of these labels predicts exam weightage.

Check a unit after you can explain it and complete its relevant example/proof. Book mapping and source-file links are in [README.md](README.md#slide-to-book-roadmap). Slide references use numbered slides. Important wording corrections are in [Reading cautions](README.md#reading-cautions-for-the-existing-slides).

## Scope boundary

The five heading categories above are the current Linear Algebra plan. Introductory references to tensors, Taylor expansions, economics, fixed points and Gauss-Jacobi are recorded as orientation, not promoted to full new units. Course announcements, textbook covers, blank pages and repeated continuation slides are not separate mathematical topics.
